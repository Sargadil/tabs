RTL horizontal navigation

In a right-to-left context, ArrowLeft / ArrowRight are reversed so the key pointing toward the next tab visually always selects it. Direction is read from the DOM — there is no options.rtl flag. Here a wrapping <div dir="rtl"> is enough (a <html dir="rtl"> document works too).
