Cancelable tab switching

tabs:beforechange fires before the selection changes, whatever triggered it (mouse, keyboard, selectTab()). Call preventDefault() and nothing moves — no ARIA/tabindex/hidden/focus change, and tabs:change never fires.

 

Editor

Tick the box above, then try to switch tabs — the change is blocked.

Preview

Untick it and switching works again.
