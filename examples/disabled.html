Disabled tabs

A disabled tab cannot be activated (click, Enter, Space) and is skipped by arrow keys, Home/End, and wrap-around. There is no library-specific attribute — you use whatever a native <button> or a custom role="tab" element already uses.

Default nav

Mark the panel's .tab-panel__title with aria-disabled="true"; the generated tab becomes a real <button disabled>.
